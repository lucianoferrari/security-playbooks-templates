## Presenter notes

- Open with the one thing you want directors to remember.
- Translate technical risk into business consequence: revenue, customers, regulatory exposure, operational downtime.
- Never surprise the audit committee chair — preview material issues before the meeting.
- Be explicit about what you are **not** asking for; it builds trust for when you do ask.
